- Circulation / sales figures: unverified (ideas.md) → not mentioned.
- "Everyone believed it": qualified to believers and skeptics both reading on (LOC).
- Authorship: "historians generally credit" Locke, not stated as proven fact.
- Third source (loc.gov catalogue record) returned 403 → not cited.
