### Myths and unverified claims kept out of the script
- "Sank in 20 minutes" (also in the working title): not on any museum page; replaced by "within minutes".
- The king ordered a second gun deck at the last minute; asymmetric hull (different measuring rulers); a salute "sank" it. None of these is verified by the museum, so none is used.
- "50 dead" as a single figure (see #32).
- The wreck was "forgotten" for 300 years (see #46).
- 64 guns aboard on the day (it sailed with 56, per MOW).
- PEG for "26 years" (see #59).
